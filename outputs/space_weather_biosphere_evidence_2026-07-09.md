## Second Search Pass: Early Biodetector Candidates

Tools used: Acumen, Tavily, Parallel Search, Exa.

### Elodea Oxygen Production

- `The decrease in diurnal oxygen production in Elodea under the influence of high geomagnetic variability`, Elizabeth Davies, 2023, `International Journal of Biometeorology`.
- Status: `verified / promising plant-biodetector`.
- The study monitored dissolved oxygen, light, temperature, air pressure, and geomagnetic-field data in an electromagnetically quiet setting.
- It reported reduced oxygen/light-normalized production and altered lag between peak light and peak oxygen during high geomagnetic variability.
- This is a stronger "early biodetector" candidate than generic SIF because the measured output is local, continuous, and metabolically direct.

### Lima Bean Photosynthesis / ROS / Magnetoreception

- `The Geomagnetic Field (GMF) Is Required for Lima Bean Photosynthesis and Reactive Oxygen Species Production`, Parmagnani et al., 2023, `International Journal of Molecular Sciences`.
- Status: `verified mechanism support`.
- Near-null magnetic-field conditions altered photosynthesis-related structure/function, RubisCO, chlorophyll/carotenoids, ROS, and MagR/IScA-related gene expression.
- This supports the plausibility of plant magnetic sensitivity through photosynthesis, ROS, iron-sulfur / MagR-like mechanisms, and cryptochrome-linked signaling.

### Fungal Machines / Mycelial Biosensors

- `Fungal Machines: Sensing and Computing with Fungi`, Springer, 2023, edited by Andrew Adamatzky.
- Status: `verified fungal sensing substrate`, not direct space-weather evidence.
- Exa surfaced the book and related work on fungal electrical activity, fungal wearables, moisture sensing, pressure sensing, photosensors, electrical signal transfer, and computing prototypes.
- Key practical implication: mycelium is a plausible multi-modal sensor substrate, but a space-weather detector requires a controlled protocol and confound handling.

### Methodological Shift

The strongest experimental framing is not "one organism predicts storms" but a multi-channel time-series design:

`space-weather indices -> plant metabolic/SIF/oxygen signals -> bacterial luminescence -> fungal electrophysiology -> local environmental controls -> model/system-state annotations`

Early-warning claims should be evaluated by lead-lag analysis and cross-validation against NOAA/SWPC, Kp/Ap/Dst, solar wind, local weather, light, temperature, humidity, and equipment noise.
